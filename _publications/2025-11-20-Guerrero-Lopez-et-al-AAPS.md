This proceedings paper presents **DA-MARTA**, a domain-adversarial Gaussian Mixture Variational Autoencoder for interpretable phonemic grouping across speech corpora and dialects. It is a separate contribution from the MARTA journal article, extending the research to domain adaptation and cross-dialect evaluation.

Springer published the chapter online on **20 November 2025** and assigns **2026** as the bibliographic year of the proceedings volume. The workshop took place on **21–22 November 2024**.

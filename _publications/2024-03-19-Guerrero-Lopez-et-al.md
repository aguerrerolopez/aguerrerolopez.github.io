**Accepted for publication in Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence.** Acceptance reported on 24 September 2026; final publication details are forthcoming.

This first-author paper, known as **MARTA**, comes from my postdoctoral research at **Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)** in the Bioengineering and Optoelectronics Group.

The model addresses automatic phonemic grouping of Parkinsonian speech.

## Preprint

The earlier version is available on [TechRxiv](https://doi.org/10.36227/techrxiv.171084943.31044695/v1) under the title “MARTA: a model for the automatic phonemic grouping of the parkinsonian speech”. This DOI identifies the preprint, not the accepted journal article.

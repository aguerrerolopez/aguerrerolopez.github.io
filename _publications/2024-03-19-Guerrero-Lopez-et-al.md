**Published in Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, 184, 116378 (2026).** The article is available online and assigned to the 15 November 2026 issue.

[Read the published article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2026.116378).

This first-author paper, known as **MARTA**, comes from my postdoctoral research at **Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM)** in the Bioengineering and Optoelectronics Group.

The model addresses automatic phonemic grouping of Parkinsonian speech.

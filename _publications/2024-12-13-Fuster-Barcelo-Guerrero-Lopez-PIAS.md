This proceedings paper examines doctoral funding in Spain in relation to national salaries, inflation, and housing affordability, using data on FPU and PIF scholarships.

Presented at **PIAS 2024**, held on **13 December 2024** in Madrid, Spain. Published in the symposium proceedings, **pages 7–10**, ISSN **3045-6657**, under a **CC BY 4.0** licence.

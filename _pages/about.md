Overview
------
I am a **postdoctoral researcher** at the [Institute of Medical Microbiology (IMM), University of Zurich (UZH)](https://www.imm.uzh.ch/de/Highlights.html), working in [Prof. Dr. Adrian Egli](https://appliedmicrobiologyresearch.net/en/)'s group. From **July 2026**, I will hold a **Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship (MSCA PF)** for **OUTBRAID**, a project on AI-driven outbreak detection from multimodal microbiology data.

My long-term goal is to build an independent research group in **applied machine learning for health and medical data science**. I work on clinically grounded AI problems together with domain experts, with a focus on **multimodal clinical decision support**, **trustworthy biomedical AI**, and **open-source tools and datasets** that make advanced methods usable for clinicians, microbiologists, and biomedical researchers.

Research Program
------
- **Multimodal clinical decision support.** I develop machine learning methods that integrate heterogeneous biomedical data and expert knowledge to support diagnosis, surveillance, and treatment-related decisions.
- **Trustworthy biomedical AI.** I am interested in interpretable, reliable, and clinically useful AI systems that can be understood and evaluated by the professionals who use them.
- **Open-source software and data resources.** I build reusable tools, applications, and public datasets that lower the barrier for applying AI in real health workflows.

Selected Outputs
------
- [MARISMa: a routine MALDI-TOF MS database from 2018 to 2024](/publication/2025-06-01-Schmidt-Santiago-et-al/) and the associated [dataset](/publication/2025-06-02-Schmidt-Santiago-et-al-dataset/): a large-scale open microbiology resource designed to support method development, benchmarking, and translational work on MALDI-TOF data.
- [Automatic antibiotic resistance prediction in *Klebsiella pneumoniae* based on MALDI-TOF mass spectra](/publication/2023-02-01-Guerrero-Lopez-et-al/): an example of clinically driven machine learning for rapid resistance prediction from routine microbiology measurements.
- [Automated web-based typing of *Clostridioides difficile* ribotypes via MALDI-TOF MS](/publication/2025-07-24-Blazquez-Sanchez-et-al/): a translational project aimed at turning machine learning into an accessible microbiology application.
- [NeuroVoz: a Castillian Spanish corpus of parkinsonian speech](/publication/2024-03-04-Mendes-Laureano-et-al/) and [MARTA](/publication/2024-03-19-Guerrero-Lopez-et-al/): open resources for speech-based digital biomarkers and machine learning in Parkinson's disease.
- [Automatic semantic segmentation of the osseous structures of the paranasal sinuses](/publication/2025-05-01-Sun-et-al/): applied medical imaging work on clinically relevant CT segmentation.
- [Exploring the Power of Photoplethysmogram Matrix for Atrial Fibrillation Detection with Integrated Explainability](/publication/2024-03-21-Fuster-Barcelo-et-al/): a health AI project centered on explainability and real-world interpretability.

Funding, Leadership, and Current Activities
------
- **MSCA PF awardee (2026-2028).** I was awarded a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship for **OUTBRAID**, focused on AI-driven outbreak detection with multimodal microbiology data.
- **Supervision and mentoring.** I have supervised **1 BSc thesis**, **8 MSc theses**, and I currently **co-supervise 4 PhD students**.
- **Teaching and training.** I have contributed to undergraduate, master's, and professional training in machine learning and health data science, and I currently participate as faculty in the **ESCMID AI and Machine Learning in Medical Microbiology Diagnostics** course.
- **Scientific community engagement.** My recent activities include invited talks, participation in international workshops, and contributions to community-facing open science efforts in biomedical AI.
- **Open-source translational development.** I am currently developing an **AST application** to turn AI models into a practical, reusable tool for microbiology workflows.

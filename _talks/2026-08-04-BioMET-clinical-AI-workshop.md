I designed and delivered a hands-on session on reproducing a clinical machine-learning study with Orange Data Mining. Participants explored EHR-derived tabular data, missingness and class imbalance, built visual machine-learning workflows, and interpreted diagnostic metrics including sensitivity, specificity, positive predictive value, and negative predictive value.

The complete teaching materials are freely available as an open-access lesson on Zenodo: [*Predicting negative blood cultures from EHR using Orange Data Mining*](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21738591) (CC BY 4.0).

The session formed part of the [BioMET '26](https://vit.ac.in/conference/biomet-2026/) pre-conference workshop [*Clinical AI Transforming Diagnosis, Treatment & Beyond*](https://vit.ac.in/conference/biomet-2026/program.html).
